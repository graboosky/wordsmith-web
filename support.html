Support

Answers to the questions people ask most, and how to reach us.

Which iPhones does Wordsmith work on?

Wordsmith's coach runs on Apple Intelligence, so it needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with it turned on in Settings, under Apple Intelligence & Siri, and iOS 27 or later.

Why is a source marked "Draft"?

Its takeaways were found by the model on your iPhone, reading in parts. Keep what's useful and skip the rest.

Can Wordsmith read a YouTube video?

Not from a link yet. Either open the video's transcript on YouTube, copy it, and paste it into Add a source, or tap Listen in Add a source and play the video out loud, on your iPhone or another device. Wordsmith keeps listening while you switch apps.

Adding from another app

In Safari, Photos or any app that can share, tap Share, then Wordsmith. It takes a link, a screenshot or text, and says "Saved to Wordsmith". The next time you open Wordsmith, Add a source opens with it and finds its takeaways.

Speaking isn't working

Check that Wordsmith may use the microphone (Settings, Wordsmith, Microphone). The first answer may wait while your iPhone downloads its speech model. You can always type an answer instead.

Restoring or managing a subscription

In Wordsmith, open Settings from the Today tab, then choose Restore purchases or Manage subscription. Subscriptions can also be managed in the iPhone's Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/graboosky/wordsmith-web/issues. Please don't include anything private: issues are public.
