Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 September 2026. This page describes what the Wordsmith app for iPhone does with what you give it.

In short: Wordsmith has no account, no server of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What you keep stays on your iPhone. Your voice is turned into text on your iPhone and the recording is deleted straight away.

What stays on your iPhone

Your notebook, the sources you add and their text, your notes, your practice history and streak, and your answers to the first questions the app asks. They are stored only on your iPhone, are not synced, and are deleted when you delete the app.

Your voice

The microphone is on only while you answer a practice prompt. Your answer is recorded to a temporary file, turned into text on your iPhone by Apple's speech recognition, and the recording is deleted as soon as that's done. Recordings never leave your iPhone.

Apple Intelligence

Feedback on your answers, and finding the phrases worth keeping in a source, run on Apple's on-device model. When Wordsmith uses Apple's larger model through Private Cloud Compute, the text of a source is sent to Apple's servers for that request only; Apple designs Private Cloud Compute so that it does not keep that data. Wordsmith does not send your data to any other AI service.

Links you add

When you add a web link, your iPhone fetches the page directly from that website, as a browser would, without cookies. When you add a post from X, its text comes from X's public embed service (publish.twitter.com). Those sites receive an ordinary request from your iPhone, including its IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Screenshots

The text in a screenshot you add is read on your iPhone. The picture itself is not stored.

Reminders and purchases

The daily reminder is scheduled on your iPhone. Subscriptions are handled by Apple; Wordsmith never sees your payment details.

Children

Wordsmith is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If what the app does with your information changes, this page changes with the same release. Questions: see Support.
